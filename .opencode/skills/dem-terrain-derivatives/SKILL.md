# DEM Terrain Derivatives

Part of the **Bhajanlal** model-building skill library. Pillar: 112. Satellite & Geospatial Engineering (50 skills)

Use when generating slope, aspect, hillshade, and watershed accumulation layers from SRTM/Copernicus DEMs.

## Workflow

1. Load DEM.
2. Compute slope/aspect/hillshade.
3. Derive watersheds.
4. Handle edge effects.
5. Export derivatives.

## Output

Terrain derivative layers.
