# dynamic-fog-and-atmosphere-clear

Part of the **Bhajanlal** model-building skill library. Pillar: 105. God's Eye View - OpenCode Cesium/GLSL/Node Skills (100 skills)

Use when stripping haze, Rayleigh scattering, and atmospheric fog at low altitudes for sharper ground views.

## Workflow

1. Disable or reduce fog/atmosphere at low altitude.
2. Expose a toggle for clear optics.
3. Preserve atmosphere at high altitude.
4. Validate ground sharpness.

## Output

Haze-free low-altitude ground views.
