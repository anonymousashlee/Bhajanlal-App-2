# Pansharpening Fusion

Part of the **Bhajanlal** model-building skill library. Pillar: 112. Satellite & Geospatial Engineering (50 skills)

Use when fusing high-resolution panchromatic bands with lower-resolution multispectral imagery.

## Workflow

1. Co-register PAN and MS.
2. Choose fusion method (IHS/GS/Brovey).
3. Fuse and restore colors.
4. Evaluate spectral fidelity.
5. Export sharpened product.

## Output

Pansharpened imagery.
