# Human-in-the-Loop Forensic Verification Workflows

Part of the **Bhajanlal** model-building skill library. Pillar: 103. Satellite & Aerial Remote-Sensing Imaging (100 skills)

Use when presenting side-by-side raw, sharpened, and temporal-reference crops to prevent false identification.

## Workflow

1. Assemble a triptych per candidate - raw, enhanced, and independent reference.
2. Annotate which pixels are sensor-grounded versus AI-generated.
3. Require an analyst to confirm identity with a stated confidence level.
4. Log every decision with the evidence shown for later audit.

## Output

Auditable human-verified identification with full evidence trail.
