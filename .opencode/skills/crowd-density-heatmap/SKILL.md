# Crowd Density Heatmap

Part of the **Bhajanlal** model-building skill library. Pillar: 111. CCTV & Video Surveillance Engineering (50 skills)

Use when generating spatial occupancy heatmaps and headcount estimates from overhead feeds.

## Workflow

1. Detect or count people per frame.
2. Accumulate counts into a spatial grid.
3. Render heatmap overlays.
4. Estimate total headcount.
5. Alert on density thresholds.

## Output

Crowd density heatmap and counts.
