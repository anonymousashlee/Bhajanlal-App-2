# Audio Two-Way SIP Bridge

Part of the **Bhajanlal** model-building skill library. Pillar: 111. CCTV & Video Surveillance Engineering (50 skills)

Use when integrating G.711/AAC two-way audio channels from intercom cameras.

## Workflow

1. Negotiate SIP/RTP session with the camera.
2. Configure G.711/AAC codecs and jitter buffers.
3. Bridge audio to the operator client.
4. Handle echo cancellation and gain control.
5. Monitor audio quality and dropouts.

## Output

Two-way audio bridge for intercom cameras.
