# SGP4 Orbit Propagator

Part of the **Bhajanlal** model-building skill library. Pillar: 112. Satellite & Geospatial Engineering (50 skills)

Use when computing satellite ECI/ECEF positions and ground tracks using Skyfield or sgp4.

## Workflow

1. Load TLE.
2. Propagate with SGP4.
3. Convert to ECI/ECEF.
4. Generate ground track.
5. Export positions.

## Output

Satellite orbit positions.
