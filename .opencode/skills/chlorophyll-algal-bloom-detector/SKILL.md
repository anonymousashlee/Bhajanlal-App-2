Part of the **Bhajanlal** model-building skill library. Pillar: 112. Satellite & Geospatial Engineering (50 skills)

## Workflow

1. Compute chlorophyll indices.
2. Threshold bloom areas.
3. Track temporal changes.
4. Validate with in-situ data.
5. Export alerts.
