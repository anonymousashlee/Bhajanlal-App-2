# Camera Tamper/Blur Detector

Part of the **Bhajanlal** model-building skill library. Pillar: 111. CCTV & Video Surveillance Engineering (50 skills)

Use when detecting lens obstruction, spray-paint occlusion, or sudden field-of-view shifts.

## Workflow

1. Monitor frame brightness and edge energy.
2. Detect blur, occlusion, or shift.
3. Compare against reference frame.
4. Alert on tamper events.
5. Log and snapshot evidence.

## Output

Camera tamper detection.
