# PPE Safety Compliance

Part of the **Bhajanlal** model-building skill library. Pillar: 111. CCTV & Video Surveillance Engineering (50 skills)

Use when detecting hardhats, high-vis vests, and safety gear in industrial camera feeds.

## Workflow

1. Detect persons and PPE items.
2. Associate PPE to each person.
3. Evaluate compliance per zone.
4. Log violations with snapshots.
5. Generate compliance reports.

## Output

PPE compliance monitoring.
