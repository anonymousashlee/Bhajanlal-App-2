# Single-Image HDR Tone Mapping (Reinhard/Drago)

Part of the **Bhajanlal** model-building skill library. Pillar: 104. God's Eye View - Image Clarity & Car/Human Detection (100 skills)

Use when extreme dynamic range must be compressed.

## Workflow

1. Estimate HDR from single LDR image.
2. Apply Reinhard or Drago tone mapping.
3. Preserve detail in sunlit roads and dark underpasses.
4. Tune for vehicle visibility.

## Output

Tone-mapped frames with balanced exposure.
