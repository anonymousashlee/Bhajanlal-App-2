# H.265 Transcode Archiver

Part of the **Bhajanlal** model-building skill library. Pillar: 111. CCTV & Video Surveillance Engineering (50 skills)

Use when batch-transcoding older H.264 archives to HEVC/AV1 to reduce cold storage costs.

## Workflow

1. Scan archive for H.264 candidates.
2. Transcode to HEVC/AV1 with quality targets.
3. Verify integrity and metadata.
4. Replace originals after validation.
5. Report space savings.

## Output

Cold-storage transcoding pipeline.
