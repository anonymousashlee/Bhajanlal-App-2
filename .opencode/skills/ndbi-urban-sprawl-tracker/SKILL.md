# NDBI Urban Sprawl Tracker

Part of the **Bhajanlal** model-building skill library. Pillar: 112. Satellite & Geospatial Engineering (50 skills)

Use when quantifying built-up impervious surfaces and urban expansion over multi-year intervals.

## Workflow

1. Compute NDBI per date.
2. Threshold built-up areas.
3. Compare multi-year extents.
4. Quantify expansion.
5. Visualize change.

## Output

Urban sprawl change analysis.
