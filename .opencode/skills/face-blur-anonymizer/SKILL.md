# Face Blur Anonymizer

Part of the **Bhajanlal** model-building skill library. Pillar: 111. CCTV & Video Surveillance Engineering (50 skills)

Use when applying real-time Gaussian blur to detected faces for GDPR/privacy compliance.

## Workflow

1. Detect faces in frame.
2. Apply Gaussian blur to ROIs.
3. Handle tracking to keep blur stable.
4. Preserve audit logs.
5. Configure blur strength and zones.

## Output

Privacy-compliant blurred video.
