# License Plate Redactor

Part of the **Bhajanlal** model-building skill library. Pillar: 111. CCTV & Video Surveillance Engineering (50 skills)

Use when automatically masking vehicle plates in public-facing or shared video exports.

## Workflow

1. Detect and track plates.
2. Apply mask/blur to plate regions.
3. Render redacted export.
4. Keep original secured.
5. Log redaction actions.

## Output

Redacted video exports.
