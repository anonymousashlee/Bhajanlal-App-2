# SAR Sentinel-1 Preprocessor

Part of the **Bhajanlal** model-building skill library. Pillar: 112. Satellite & Geospatial Engineering (50 skills)

Use when performing radiometric calibration, speckle filtering, and terrain correction on SAR data.

## Workflow

1. Apply orbit and calibration.
2. Convert to backscatter (dB).
3. Filter speckle.
4. Terrain-correct with DEM.
5. Export analysis-ready product.

## Output

Analysis-ready SAR product.
